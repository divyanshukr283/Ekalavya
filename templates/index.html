Our Mission

Why Early Intervention Matters

Millions of children in underserved communities drop out of school every year — not because they lack potential, but because no one noticed the warning signs in time.

Ekalavya was built for educators, volunteers, NGO field workers, and community education coordinators who work with government schools and rural learning centres. By identifying at-risk students early, we help communities direct their limited resources exactly where they're needed most.
